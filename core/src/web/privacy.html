Privacy

How Sate handles your data. Last updated 1 August 2026.

Sate is a nutrition and activity tracker. It necessarily holds health information about you. This page describes exactly what is stored, what leaves the service, and how to get it back or erase it.

What Sate stores

  • Meals you log — description, photo (when you use the camera), estimated calories and macros.
  • Activity you log or import — type, duration, distance, calories burned.
  • Body data you provide — weight and weigh-in history, height, date of birth, biological sex, activity level. These are the inputs to the calorie and macro targets.
  • Your goals, plans and scheduled meals.
  • Coach conversations and check-in messages.
  • Your email address and sign-in identity.

Apple Health

On iOS, and only if you connect it, Sate reads workouts, active energy, walking/running and cycling distance, heart rate, body mass, date of birth and biological sex. Heart rate is read so a period of activity can be turned into a logged workout. Sate writes back only your weigh-ins, and only if you switch that on. Nothing else is written to Apple Health.

What is sent to other services

  • AI provider (Google Gemini). When you log by text or photo, the meal text or image is sent for estimation. When you use the coach, it also receives the figures it is advising on: your first name, sex, age, height, current weight, activity level, calculated energy needs, your weight goals and your recent average intake. Photo and text estimation do not include your name or identifiers.
  • Nutrition databases. Barcode and food lookups query Open Food Facts, USDA FoodData Central and similar services. These receive the barcode or the search term — not your identity or your health data.
  • Sign-in. Handled by Firebase Authentication (Google). Sate sees your email address and a user identifier.
  • Payments. Handled by Stripe. Sate never sees or stores card details.
  • Entitlements. Your email address is checked against a shared subscription service to determine which features your plan includes.

Sate does not sell your data, does not share it for advertising, and contains no advertising or analytics trackers.

Where it is stored

In Google Cloud (Firestore and Cloud Run) in the United States, under an account controlled by the operator of this instance. Your records are stored under your own user identifier and are not readable by other users of the app.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Sate does not expire your history — a food diary is only useful with its past intact.

Getting your data out, and deleting it

In the app, open Settings → Your data:

  • Download my data gives you a single JSON file containing every record stored under your account.
  • Delete my account data permanently erases your meals, activity, weigh-ins, goals, plans and coach messages. It cannot be undone. It does not delete your sign-in identity, so signing in again gives you a fresh, empty account.

Deleting your data does not remove foods you contributed to the shared food database, because those entries are shared reference data rather than a record about you.

Self-hosting

Sate is also available as an open-source, self-hosted edition. If you run your own instance, your data lives on your own infrastructure and this page does not apply to it — you are the operator, and you supply your own AI provider keys.

Questions about your data, or want it removed on your behalf? Contact the operator of this instance.
